About

pyRing is a Python package for black hole ringdown analysis, model comparison, and parameter estimation of post-merger gravitational-wave signals. It supports ringdown analyses with a variety of waveform models, parametrised tests of General Relativity, and quasi-normal mode spectrum predictions. The code is used within the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA collaboration to produce catalogs of black hole ringdown properties and tests of GR in the merger-ringdown regime.

This workshop brings together the pyRing user and developer community for a focused, practical day of talks and tutorials, immediately following the Ringdown in Lisbon meeting. Further details on the program and registration will be posted here as they are finalized.
