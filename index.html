About

pyRing is a Python package for black hole ringdown analysis, model comparison, and parameter estimation of post-merger gravitational-wave signals. It supports ringdown analyses with a range of waveform models, parametrised tests of General Relativity, and quasi-normal mode spectrum predictions for emissions alternative to the Kerr solution. The code is used within the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA collaboration to produce catalogs of black hole ringdown properties and tests of GR in the merger-ringdown regime.

This workshop brings the pyRing user and developer community together for a focused, practical day of talks and tutorials, immediately following the Ringdown in Lisbon meeting. Details on the program and registration will be posted here as they are finalised.
