pyRing Workshop

A one-day, hands-on workshop on black hole ringdown analysis with pyRing, held as a satellite event following Ringdown in Lisbon.

Date  22 October 2026
Duration  1 day
Venue  Room P12, Physics building, IST Alameda, Lisbon
Register for the workshop

Places are limited — registration is confirmed by the organisers.

About

This workshop brings the pyRing user and developer community together for a focused, practical day of talks and tutorials, immediately following the Ringdown in Lisbon meeting. The program is below.
